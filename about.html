about me

christopher e. morales soriano

Based in Greater Boston, I work across hardware engineering, RF and analog video experiments, graphic design, and still/motion photography.

I like taking older machines, circuits, and formats apart to understand how they work—and building tools or media around them.

This site documents both finished work and raw bench experiments under @cemso and @kelsoitin.
