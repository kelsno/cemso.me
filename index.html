You're reading this because you know that preserving and engineering classic hardware requires more than off-the-shelf solutions. You understand the value of trusting an engineer who speaks fluent RF, analog video, and vintage circuitry.

I specialize in hardware engineering, specifically through radio and television. In short, my passion is to resuscitate the olden technology that many of us (rightfully so) have left in the past, and give them a sort of new beginning.

My creative services also include logo and branding design, as well as graphic design for marketing, promotional materials -- along with videography and photography.
